Jeju Dining — a 3D Korean restaurant game

Jeju Dining (제주 다이닝) is a free time-management game you play in the browser. You run a small traditional Korean hanok diner with a red-brick kitchen, a tiled roof and a garden courtyard, working alongside a chef while guests arrive at the door.

How to play

  1. Seat: click an empty stool while a guest is waiting at the entrance.
  2. Order: click the seated guest once they have chosen a dish.
  3. Cook: the chef cooks the order at the stove and puts the plate on the pass.
  4. Serve: click the finished plate, then the guest it belongs to, before their patience runs out.
  5. Guests who are served quickly leave a tip. Each service lasts three minutes.

Levels

  • Level 1, The counter — daily target ₩20,000.
  • Level 2, The courtyard — string lights and a garden table; target ₩35,000.
  • Level 3, The deck — a timber deck under a vine pergola; target ₩55,000.
  • Level 4, The pyeongsang — a raised wooden platform with floor cushions; target ₩80,000.
  • Level 5, Night market — red lanterns and a third courtyard table; target ₩110,000.

Menu

Bibimbap (₩9,000), ramyeon (₩6,000), kimbap (₩5,000) and tteokbokki (₩7,000), with bulgogi (₩12,000) unlocked in the market.

Market upgrades

Spend what you earn on a courtyard table, a twin-burner stove, warm lanterns, a street signboard and a bulgogi menu.

Features

Day and night lighting, procedural lofi music, a level board to replay any level you have reached, and a diner you can name yourself. Progress is saved in your browser; no account or download needed.
